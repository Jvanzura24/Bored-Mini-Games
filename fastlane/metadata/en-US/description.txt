Bored Mini Games is a collection of fast, simple games you can play anytime you have a few minutes.

Jump into arcade classics, puzzles, memory challenges, word games, math games, reaction tests, card games, and quick brain teasers from one lightweight app. Each game is designed to be easy to start, quick to replay, and useful for short breaks.

Games include:

- Tic-Tac-Toe, Snake, Solitaire, Paddle Ball, Brick Break, and Four in a Row
- Memory Match, Pattern Recall, Grid Flash, Tap Sequence, and Memory Digits
- Math Rush, Prime Rush, Quick Multiply, Factor Find, Sum Target, and Powers Quiz
- Word Scramble, Word Chain, Sentence Order, Anagram Pick, Spelling Check, and more
- Color Flood, Lights Out, Slide Puzzle, Sudoku Lite, Peg Jump, and Tower of Hanoi
- Reaction Tap, Speed Tap, Target Tap, Reflex Catch, Star Dodge, and other fast-tap challenges

Features:

- Over 100 mini games in one app
- Searchable game list
- High scores
- First-time instructions for each game
- Quick rounds for casual play
- A mix of arcade, puzzle, math, word, memory, and trivia games

Whether you want a quick puzzle, a fast reflex challenge, or a simple game to pass the time, Bored Mini Games gives you a large variety of choices without needing to switch apps.
