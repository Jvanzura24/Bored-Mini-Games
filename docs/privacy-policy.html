Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 18, 2026

Bored Mini Games is a collection of quick mini games for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what information the app uses and how third-party advertising may work.

Information Stored on Your Device

The app may store gameplay preferences, viewed instructions, and high scores locally on your device using Apple-provided storage. This information is used to run app features and is not sent to the developer by the app.

Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob to show ads. Google may collect or receive device information, advertising identifiers, approximate location, app activity, diagnostics, and other data as described in Google's policies. AdMob may use this information to provide, limit, measure, and improve advertising.

Learn more about Google's advertising data practices at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request advertising consent and provide privacy choices. If privacy options are required in your region, the app shows a privacy options button.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience. The app does not ask users to create an account or intentionally collect personal information directly from children.

Data Sharing

The developer does not sell personal information collected directly by the app. Advertising data handled by Google AdMob is governed by Google's policies and your consent choices where applicable.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when app features, advertising behavior, or legal requirements change. Updates will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the App Store listing or the public GitHub repository for this app.
